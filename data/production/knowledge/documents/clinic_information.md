# Clinic Information

Maple Crescent Family Clinic is a family healthcare clinic at 24 Maple Crescent, Garden View District, Lahore, Pakistan. Clinic ID: CLINIC-001. Tenant ID: TENANT-001. The clinic provides general practice, family medicine, cardiology, dermatology, pediatrics, preventive care and health screening services. English is the primary language; Urdu and Roman Urdu are supported for communication.
